Fire show

Fire

Live fire moving to music. Poi, staff, snakes and fire breathing woven into one performance.

What it is

A fire performance with poi, staff and snakes, finished with fire breathing. The length and program are built around your event, from a short finale to a full set.

What it gives your event

The moment guests remember and film. It works at festivals, weddings, birthdays, corporate evenings, retreats and music events.

No two shows are the same. I build each one around the venue, the music and the mood of the evening, or perform to your own brief.

Contact juggling

Flow

A single clear sphere that seems to float on its own. Quiet, hypnotic and close enough to touch.

What it is

Contact juggling with one acrylic ball: the sphere rolls across hands, arms and body without ever being thrown. It works on stage and right next to your guests.

What it gives your event

A perfect fit where open fire isn't allowed: indoor venues, dinners, retreats, presentations. Calm, elegant and fascinating at any age.

What makes it different

Contact juggling is one of the rarest disciplines in flow art. Most guests have never seen it live, and it can hold a whole room in silence.

  • Stage
  • Among guests
  • Indoors
  • No fire
LED show

Light

Light instead of flame. Glowing props paint patterns and colour in the air in time with the music.

What it is

An LED performance with poi, staff and snakes. Bright, colourful and completely safe.

What it gives your event

Ideal for dark halls, clubs, children's parties and any venue where open fire is off limits.

What makes it different

Colours and pace are chosen for your event, from a calm glow to a full psychedelic set.

  • LED poi
  • LED staff
  • Indoors
  • Safe for kids

Questions

How much does a show cost?

It depends on the length and format. Send a request and I'll reply with a personal offer in USD.

Can the show be tailored to us?

Yes. I can perform to your brief or build a program around the length, format and mood of your event.

Can you perform indoors?

Yes. Contact juggling and the LED show work in any hall. Fire indoors is possible only if the venue allows it.

What do you need from the venue?

A performance area, a fire extinguisher, a place to change and a sound system. Anything else depends on the show format.

What if it rains?

Light rain is fine for fire. In heavy rain we move under cover or switch to the LED show or contact juggling.
