Fire

Fire show
Watch in full

Live fire moving to music. Poi, staff, snakes and fire breathing woven into one performance.

What it is

A fire show with poi, fire staff or snakes. Fire breathing throughout the performance adds a spectacle that leaves no one indifferent.

What makes it different

No two shows are the same. I build each one around the venue, the music and the mood of the evening, or perform to your own brief. It's the moment guests remember and film.

Where it fits

Festivals, weddings, birthdays, corporate events, retreats and music events. Outdoors or at any venue that allows open fire.

Flow

Contact juggling
Watch in full

A clear sphere that seems to float on its own. Quiet, hypnotic and close enough to touch.

What it is

Contact juggling with an acrylic sphere: it rolls across hands, arms and body without ever being thrown.

What makes it different

Contact juggling is one of the rarest disciplines in flow art, and most guests have never seen it live. Calm and elegant, it fascinates at any age and can hold a whole room in silence.

Where it fits

Restaurants, dinners, retreats, presentations and exhibitions: anywhere open fire isn't allowed. On stage or right among your guests, indoors or outdoors.

  • Stage
  • Among guests
  • Indoors
  • No fire

Light

LED show
Watch in full

Light instead of flame. Glowing props paint patterns and colour in the air in time with the music.

What it is

A light show with LED poi. Bright, colourful and completely safe: no flame, no smoke.

What makes it different

Colours and pace are chosen for your event, from a calm glow to a full psychedelic set.

Where it fits

Dark halls, clubs, parties, children's events and any venue where open fire is off limits.

  • LED poi
  • Indoors
  • Safe for kids

Questions

How much does a show cost?

It depends on the length and format. Send a request and I'll reply with a personal offer in USD.

Can the show be tailored to us?

Yes. I can perform to your brief or build a program around the length, format and mood of your event.

Can you perform indoors?

Yes. Contact juggling and the LED show work in any hall. Fire indoors is possible only if the venue allows it.

What do you need from the venue?

A performance area, a fire extinguisher, a place to change and a sound system. Anything else depends on the show format.

What if it rains?

Light rain is fine for fire. In heavy rain we move under cover or switch to the LED show or contact juggling.
